## Why is a website still important when you have Instagram?

Instagram can show your personality, but your website is the place you control. It can explain your classes, prices, location and booking options in a format students can find in search and use when they are ready to commit.

## Which languages should a Swiss yoga website offer?

Start with the language your students use most, then add a complete second language where it serves your audience. German and English are practical in Zurich, Basel, Bern and Lucerne; French and English work well in Geneva and Lausanne; Italian matters in Ticino. Give every language version its own URL and use hreflang annotations.

## What should every yoga website include?

- Your yoga style and who it is for, such as beginners, prenatal, Vinyasa or Yin.
- A current schedule or clear availability for private sessions.
- Simple booking, prices and trial or introductory options.
- Your location, travel details and contact information.
- A teacher bio, real photographs and student reviews.

## How should booking and payment work?

A studio may embed its existing booking platform; a teacher may only need a simple enquiry form or booking link. In either case, make the path from class details to booking clear on a phone. Show prices in CHF and choose payment options that fit the business, including TWINT where it is available through the chosen provider.

## For solo yoga teachers

A freelance teacher does not need a studio-sized website. A focused one-page site can work beautifully when it has a clear teacher bio, private-session and workshop booking, online or retreat offerings, and a link-in-bio-friendly route from social media. The goal is to make it simple for someone who has just discovered you to understand what you teach and book.

## What do Swiss data protection rules mean for your site?

Make the tools on your site transparent: explain how enquiries, analytics and booking systems process data, provide a clear privacy policy, and publish accurate business contact details. Swiss and EU privacy requirements can differ by audience and activity, so have a qualified adviser review the final legal wording.

## How do you get found in Swiss search results?

- Keep your Google Business Profile complete and current.
- Use your city and neighbourhood naturally in relevant page titles and headings.
- Keep your name, address and phone number consistent across your website and listings.
- Use structured, factual content so search engines can understand your business.
- Earn relevant local mentions from wellness partners, directories and events.
