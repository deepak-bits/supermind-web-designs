Your Information Matters

Privacy Built Into Every Project

SuperMind Web Designs respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you browse our website, request a quote, or work with us on a web design and development project.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Our Commitment To Privacy

We collect only the information needed to answer questions, plan work, deliver services, and improve the experience we provide. We aim to be clear about what we collect and why, and we do not sell personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, business details, project requirements, and messages. We may also receive basic technical information, including browser type, device information, approximate location, and pages viewed, to help us improve website performance and usability.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide project estimates.
  • To deliver, maintain, and support websites and related services.
  • To improve our website, content, accessibility, security, and customer experience.
  • To communicate about an active project or service request.

Sharing And Data Security

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers only when needed to operate our website, communicate with you, host project files, or provide requested services. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can ask us to update, correct, or delete personal information you have submitted, subject to legal or contractual requirements. To make a privacy request, contact us through the Contact page.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be published on this page. If a change materially affects how we use information, we will take reasonable steps to make the update clear.
